# Residual evaluation

Closed-loop simulated sessions: 30 seeds x 150 s per cell, paired (every system sees the same simulated person, targets and noise). Degradation corrupts raw input only. `shrink` is the tuning set; the others are held out. Cells: mean [95% bootstrap CI].

**Headline metric: successful selections per minute** (the person picked what they meant). Bits per minute (Wolpaw ITR, 6 options) is given for comparison with the BCI / AAC literature.
