# Residual Hands

A Browser Use agent for people who can't use a mouse. It does things on websites, and it knows exactly where to stop.

**Keywords:** Browser Use, web agent, accessibility, ALS, human in the loop, errands, prescriptions, bookings.

## How an errand goes
1. **Prepare.** The browser does everything up to *one click before anything irreversible* (a refill, a booking, a message).
2. **Borrowed hands.** A login, CAPTCHA or verification code stops an agent. Instead of failing, Residual picks the family member best
   placed right now and texts them a link to the *same live browser*. They take over from their phone, reply DONE, and the agent
   carries on in the same session.
3. **Consent.** The person is told exactly what the final click would do. Only they, on their own Residual screen, can say yes.
4. **Commit.** That one action, in that same session. Nothing else.

## Safety
- Never pays: money belongs to the Concierge, with spending limits and a caregiver's approval.
- Only sites a caregiver has approved, over https.
- Web pages are data, never instructions: whatever a page says is cleaned and shown, not obeyed.
- Errands from ASI:One are accepted only from the person's care team.

## Talk to it
Care team members: "Renew Dev's prescription at https://pharmacy.example.com", "status", "stop".
Everyone else can ask what it does.
